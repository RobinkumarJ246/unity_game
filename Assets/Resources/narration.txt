[intro]

My name is {player}. Twenty-six. I have a job. We'll get to that. Not tonight - tonight I'm off the clock, which is the only time anyone lets me be a person.

Snowmania. Population: small enough that everyone knows whose car is parked outside whose house at 2 AM. And they don't let it go. Ever.

I live here with my best friend since school. An year ago we bought this place. This is the only house in this town which we were able to get for like 30% of the rent. We couldn't believe that such a cozy house can be this cheap, we were hesitant initially but we didn't have any other options.  And my friend, he left his delivery job and decided to start a channel in YTube, he had forty subscribers. He streams. I keep the house standing. Mostly standing.

He goes by {streamer} now. The iconic tshirt, the mimicry voices, the catchphrases, humour and his fans... the whole thing. To me he's still the kid who bet his lunch money that the internet was a career. I took the other side of that bet. I wanted the lunch.

Turns out he was right. His numbers go up every month. Slow but Steady.

Me? I borrow his gaming setup when he's asleep. Fair trade for the chores, I figure. He thinks the chores are a favour. I think the setup is a rental. We are both wrong.

Tonight's like any other night. Fridge full of his energy drinks. Some talk show nobody watches, on for nobody. The house making its little noises, like it's gossiping about us.

Nothing ever happens in Snowmania. For the record: I said that out loud. On purpose. Like an idiot.
