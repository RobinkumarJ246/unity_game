CREEPY HORROR UI SOUNDS - FREE
50 interface sound effects for survival horror and psychological horror

Rusted metal, dissonant drones, tape and VHS glitches, static, heartbeats,
cold breath, unintelligible whispers, broken music boxes, a classic
typewriter save and a game-over stinger. Unsettling, not cartoon-spooky.

- Loudness-matched by category (hovers quietest, stingers loudest)
- Softened above ~9 kHz so they stay bearable when heard hundreds of times
- Tonal parts share D Phrygian with the tritone, so nothing clashes
- Peaks at -1 dBFS or lower, clean fade-outs

HOW THESE WERE MADE
---------------------------------------------------------------
29 of the 50 sounds use CC0 (public domain) recordings from Kenney.nl as
raw material, heavily transformed and layered with original synthesis. The
other 21 are fully original synthesis (drones, dissonant clusters, bowed
metal, heartbeats, whispers, static, tape effects). The synthesis and
processing code was written with the help of an AI assistant. No AI
audio-generation model was used.
