# ============================================================
# CONTENT HOUSE - DIALOGUES (conversations with reply choices)
#
# Format - one [section] per conversation, lines in order:
#
#   line: <text>                    the other person speaks
#   choice: <option A> | <option B> your reply options (2-3, ' | ' separated)
#
# The player's chosen reply shows as their line, then the next
# 'line:' continues the conversation. Tokens {player} {streamer}
# {friend} {intruder} are replaced live.
# Conversations are triggered by talking to characters/objects in
# the world (or wired anywhere with DialogueScreen.Play("id")).
# ============================================================
