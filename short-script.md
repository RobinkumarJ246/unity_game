# CONTENT HOUSE - Short Script (Demo: Day 1)

Abstract of the playable demo, start to end. Source of truth: `Assets/Resources/days.txt`,
`dialogues.txt`, `narration.txt`, `text_replies.txt`. Update this file as the story grows.

## Setup
10:00 PM, calm night. After a darkly funny intro narration, the player (a house-mate in a
content house) gets a text from Mr. IG: *"my room. NOW. the number is moving. don't knock,
don't ask, just come."* The workplace, the big event and what is coming are never named.

## Day 1 beat sheet
1. **Talk to Mr. IG** - he turns from his monitor and chats (funny, a little mysterious). He asks for a drink.
2. **Deliver the drink** - fetch the Gamer-Juice can from the fridge (hold E), hand it to him.
3. **Pee** - hold E at any toilet in the house; then a flush.
4. **Dinner text** - text Mr. IG on the phone about dinner. His reply arrives after a delay.
5. **Call the Manager** - the rude Manager picks up and gives a short, grumpy answer.
6. **Dinner reply** - answer Mr. IG's reply.
7. **Eat** - take the leftover pizza from the fridge, sit at the TV, bite by bite (hold E per slice).
8. **Wash the dishes** - hold E at the sink; scrub animation, gentle water sound, three plates.
9. **Manager's late text** - he messages about a package arriving tonight (nothing about where from or why).
10. **Bed** - the player thinks out loud ("Okay. Bed. Finally."). Hold E to sleep.

## Jumpscare scene (the demo's finale)
- Halfway through falling asleep, the **doorbell rings** and wakes the player.
- Open the front door: fixed camera, empty porch. The player mutters *"Weird. Nobody's there... So who rang the bell, then?"*
- A few quiet seconds, then the **delivery man slides in right in front of the player** (sting, flash, shake).
- Short dialogue, he hands over the **package** and leaves.
- Text Mr. IG (he ordered it) - he replies; place the package by the front door.
- Go back to bed. Hold E: eyes slowly close. Day 1 ends.

## Ending
Closing narration cards, then the **demo end card**. Enter returns to the main menu.

## Easter eggs
Typing free text in the Messages app triggers secret replies from Mr. IG and the Manager
(compliments, "Manidha Kadavul", "MKMK", greetings, salary questions). Spamming gets shut down.
